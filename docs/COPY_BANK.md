# MatchSieve Copy Bank

**Purpose:** Working copy for product experiments. Copy here is not evidence of conversion and must be tested with real adult users.  
**Brand voice:** calm, sharp, plain English, evidence-led. Confident about the process; honest about uncertainty.  
**Primary message:** Stop guessing the odds. Examine the evidence. MatchSieve is for serious-minded adults who value research, traceable sources and reasoning on both sides.  
**Primary action for the product workspace:** Research a match.  
**Primary action for a future public marketing page:** Explore a sample report.

## 1. Master positioning

**One-sentence positioning**  
MatchSieve organises available football data around the fixture and market question you choose, showing supporting signals, counter-evidence, sources and missing information in one readable research brief.

**Short descriptor**  
Premium football research for people who value evidence.

**Core difference from free score/stat sites**  
Scores and raw statistics are ingredients. MatchSieve's proposed paid value is the market-specific organisation of available evidence, the explicit presentation of evidence against a convenient conclusion, and a source trail that makes gaps visible.

**Proof points only when the product can demonstrate them**
- The user selects the fixture and market.
- The report separates supporting and counter-signals.
- The report identifies missing information and source records present in the archive.
- Saved reports preserve the research snapshot so the user can inspect what the report was based on.

Do not say “live”, “real time”, “complete”, “verified”, “independent”, “accurate”, “AI-powered insight”, or “fully sourced” unless the exact claim is supported for that report and deployment. The current local-first foundation is not a production live-score service.

## 2. Recommended generic homepage copy

**Eyebrow**  
FOR SERIOUS-MINDED FOOTBALL RESEARCHERS

**Headline**  
Stop guessing the odds. Examine the evidence.

**Supporting copy**  
MatchSieve is a football research desk for adults who value evidence more than noise. Choose a fixture and a market question; inspect the case for it, the case against it, the source notes and what the available records cannot verify.

**Qualification line**  
This is not for everyone. MatchSieve is for people who do their own research and value a disciplined process. It is not a tipster club, a sure-win service or a shortcut to profit.

**Primary CTA**  
Explore a reviewed sample report

**Secondary CTA for the working research app**  
Research a match

Do not show both CTAs if the page is too crowded. A marketing page should lead with the sample; the signed-in or local workspace should lead with the actual research action.

**Three proof pillars**
1. **Relevant to your question** — research the market you selected, not a generic team summary.
2. **Both sides shown** — inspect signals that support and challenge the idea.
3. **Clear about the gaps** — see what the available records do not tell you.

**How it works**
1. Find the fixture.
2. Choose the market question.
3. Review the brief, evidence and limitations.

**Trust statement**
MatchSieve is a research aid, not a promise of a result. Where evidence is incomplete, the report should say so.

### Persona B — The Critical Comparator

**Visitor state:** distrusts confident claims and wants to evaluate evidence independently.

**Headline**  
More opinions are not the same as more evidence.

**Supporting copy**  
Inspect what supports a football-market argument, what pushes back and what is still unknown. MatchSieve is designed to make the reasoning easier to inspect—not to ask you to trust a pick on faith.

**CTA**  
Inspect the evidence

**Trust copy**  
No guaranteed outcomes. No invented confidence percentages. Limited data should produce a limited-evidence report, not false certainty.

**Buying trigger to test**  
The person encounters contradictory claims and uses the source trail or counter-evidence section to evaluate them.

**Do not assume**  
This segment cannot pay because free stats are available. Test price, trust, frequency and differentiation separately.

### Persona C — The Football Publisher

**Visitor state:** must publish previews, explain matches, or prepare community discussions repeatedly and on deadline.

**Headline**  
Research the match. Keep the sources. Make the argument yours.

**Supporting copy**  
Start with a structured research brief for the fixture and market you are covering. Review evidence on both sides and retain the source notes before you write your own preview or discussion.

**CTA**  
Review a sample research brief

**Secondary line**  
Use MatchSieve as a research starting point—not as a substitute for your own editorial judgement.

**Buying trigger to test**  
The publisher can reuse the format across multiple fixtures and can trace a fact when editing or checking a draft.

**Potential later offer**  
A creator plan may include a higher report allowance, multi-fixture workflows, source-preserving export and archive features. Do not sell these features before they exist and pass tests.

**Do not claim**  
“Publish in seconds”, “zero research time” or “never publish a wrong stat”. The actual time saving and error reduction are not measured.

## 4. Sample report framing

**Section title**  
See the report, not just the promise.

**Description**  
A MatchSieve report is organised around one fixture and one market question. It should make the sample size and data window visible, separate supporting signals from counter-evidence, list source information and state what is missing.

**Sample note (required when using historical data)**  
Illustrative research sample. This is a snapshot of the data recorded for the stated fixture and time; it is not a live feed. Check the source list and missing-data notes before interpreting the report.

Only use that sample note when it matches the actual sample. If synthetic/demo data are used, label it prominently as fictional and never imply the facts describe a real match. Better for commercial validation: create a real, manually reviewed historical example with permitted sources and exact timestamps.

Compliments, clicks and stated willingness are not sales. Do not introduce a cheap package merely because unqualified visitors decline the premium offer.

## 6. Objection handling

**“I can get football stats for free.”**  
You can—and you should not have to pay just to see a score or basic table. MatchSieve is for organising available evidence around a chosen market and making the counter-evidence and gaps easier to inspect.

**“How do I know the report is right?”**  
The report does not remove uncertainty. Inspect the source details, data window, sample size and missing information. If the available evidence is weak, the report should say so.

**“Does it tell me what to bet?”**  
MatchSieve helps you research a selected football market. It does not guarantee an outcome, promise profit or replace your own judgement.

**“Why a subscription?”**  
A subscription only makes sense if you repeat this research often enough to benefit from the workflow and report history. The sample should help you decide whether it fits your use; occasional users should not be pressured into paying for a habit they do not have.

**“Can I use this in my football content?”**  
Use the report as a research aid and inspect the source notes. Confirm facts against their sources before publication and add your own editorial judgement. Publisher-specific export and licensing terms must be explicit before commercial republishing.

**“Why is this report limited?”**  
The required evidence was not available in the stored records for this run. MatchSieve should show the missing inputs rather than fill the gaps with assumptions.

## 7. Lifecycle messages

### First-use welcome (email or in-app; send only under the applicable consent rules)

**Subject:** Your first MatchSieve research brief

Start with a fixture you are already looking into. Choose the market question, then read the supporting signals, evidence against and missing-data notes together. The value is not a guaranteed answer; it is a clearer view of the evidence available.

**CTA:** Research your first match

### Incomplete first report (in-app only unless the user opted into email)

You selected a fixture but have not finished the brief. Return when you are ready and choose the market you want to examine. If the archive has little relevant history, the result may be limited rather than conclusive.

**CTA:** Continue research

### Return after a quiet week (only for opted-in users; do not frame silence as failure)

Preparing another match-week preview? You can reuse the same research steps for a new fixture. Start with the market question and inspect what the current source records contain.

**CTA:** Research another fixture

### Cancellation feedback (optional, respectful)

Your subscription is set to end on [date, if that is the actual policy]. You can share the main reason so we can improve the product. This is optional; it will not delay or prevent cancellation.

Do not use loss, regret, shame, social pressure or a countdown to discourage cancellation.

## 9. Language to avoid

Never use:
- guaranteed, sure win, banker, lock, risk-free, safe bet, cannot lose;
- “make money from football”, “recover your losses”, “secure income”, “beat the bookies”;
- unsupported accuracy percentages, uncalibrated confidence, fabricated ROI or selectively reported wins;
- fake scarcity, fake countdowns, fake reviews, fake user counts or invented expert endorsements;
- false claims of live/real-time data, universal competition coverage or source verification;
- shame-inducing copy such as “Only amateurs still use free sites”.

Prefer:
- evidence, context, market question, source trail, sample size, counter-evidence, limitations, research brief;
- “what the available data shows” rather than “what will happen”;
- “research your chosen market” rather than “get winning picks”.

## 10. Copy approval checklist

Before any page, message or advert is published, verify:

- The audience and one intended action are obvious.
- The promise describes a capability that exists in the shipped build.
- Any time-saving, accuracy, customer-count or performance claim has representative evidence.
- The sample report uses permitted data and accurately states its date, scope and limitations.
- Prices, report limits, renewal, cancellation, data coverage and refund policy are visible where relevant.
- No copy targets minors, exploits financial vulnerability or implies profit is likely.
- A product owner can point to the screen/API/test that supports every product claim.
